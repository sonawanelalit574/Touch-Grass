What kind of outside

How long

The first card waits while the weights load. After that it is quicker.

Why this stays here

The card is written by Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct, an open-weight model, running in llama.cpp on this computer. Frost dates are calculated in the app. Your note is saved to data/journal.json. Nothing is sent to a model API.

A closed chatbot will happily tell Phoenix to wait for spring. The desert calendar in this app will not. Edit prompts/ranger.txt, or the voice box below, and the ranger changes. Drop another .gguf in models/ and restart to swap the model. After the download, it does not need the internet.
